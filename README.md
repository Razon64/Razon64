## 🧑‍🔬 About Me
I am a **prospective Ph.D. applicant** in Statistics and Machine Learning, completing my M.S. in Statistics at HSTU, Dinajpur (expected December 2026). My M.S. thesis, ***Customer-Complaint Drivers in Mobile Financial Services***, develops an explainable ML framework that analyses reviews to reveal which customer emotions drive complaints, switching and fraud reports.

My research sits where statistical modelling, machine learning and explainable AI meet real decisions in business, science, public health and the environment. I work as a Research Assistant at [CeMRD](https://cemrd.org/) and founded **Uexcel**, which has trained 1,700+ Bangladeshi university students for free in research methods and data analysis.
